Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Stackframe ("we", "us", or "our") collects when you visit stackframe.example, how that information is used, and the choices available to you. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect a limited amount of information automatically, and additional information only if you choose to provide it.

  • Usage data. Standard analytics information such as pages visited, time on page, approximate location derived from IP address, browser type, and referring site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Used for analytics, remembering your display preferences (such as dark or light mode), and serving advertising, as described below.
  • Information you provide directly. If you contact us through the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit.

Advertising and third-party vendors

Stackframe displays advertising served by third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use cookies for essential site functionality (such as remembering a theme preference), analytics, and advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site
  • To understand how visitors use our content, in aggregate
  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form
  • To display relevant advertising through our third-party partners

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share limited data with service providers who help us operate the site, including analytics and advertising providers, under their own respective privacy policies.

Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser, use browser extensions that block tracking, and opt out of personalized advertising through the links above. If you would like us to delete information you've submitted through the contact form, email us and we will remove it.

Children's privacy

Stackframe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
